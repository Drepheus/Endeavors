Overview

Percify is an AI avatar platform that lets creators, agencies, and brands generate realistic talking-head videos without a camera, studio, or actor. A text script becomes a lip-synced video with a photorealistic avatar, using cloned voices in 175+ languages — with watermark-free exports on every paid plan.

It started in early 2023 with a simple frustration: realistic avatars were difficult and expensive to produce. Built for accessibility, the platform has grown into an all-in-one AI video studio — recently passing 24,000 signups, with 2.5M+ avatars created, 213,500+ videos generated, and an API + MCP server that puts 100+ AI models in developers' hands.
